# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: April 26, 2026**

Your privacy is a priority. This policy explains how **Talkest** handles your information.

### 1. Information We Collect
When you sign in using Google, we collect:
- **Profile Information:** Name, Email address, and Profile Picture URL.
- **Messaging Data:** Real-time chat content stored in Firebase Firestore.
- **Device Data:** FCM Tokens to enable push notifications.

### 2. How We Use Information
The collected data is used strictly for:
- Authenticating your account.
- Facilitating real-time communication.
- Sending high-priority push notifications via Vercel proxy.

### 3. Data Integrity & Third Parties
- **No Data Selling:** I am an individual developer, not a data broker. **I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties for any reason.**
- **Service Providers:** Talkest utilizes Firebase (Google) for backend services and Vercel for notification processing. These providers have their own privacy policies.

### 4. Security Disclaimer
I implement industry-standard security measures (such as Zero-Secret Architecture and Firebase Security Rules) to protect your data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While I strive to protect your personal information, I cannot guarantee its absolute security against sophisticated breaches.

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Data is stored to ensure chat continuity. Currently, Talkest does not have an in-app "Delete Account" button. If you wish to have your data removed from the database, please contact the developer directly.
